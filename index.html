Frequently Asked Questions

I am a complete beginner with AI. Can I still do this?
+
This is exactly who this was built for. You do not need any prior experience with AI, coding, or design. The course walks you through every single step, from recording your answers to generating your page to connecting your payment system. If you can talk about your offer out loud, you can build a sales page with this system.
I do not have my offer figured out yet. Should I wait?
+
The recording process actually helps you clarify your offer. The guided prompts pull out your transformation, your audience, and your positioning as a natural byproduct of answering the questions. Many people find that building the sales page is what finally crystallizes their offer into something clear and sellable.
I have tried AI for copy before and it was terrible. How is this different?
+
Most people paste a generic prompt into ChatGPT and get generic output back. This system teaches you a specific method for working with Claude that is built on the actual structure of high-converting sales pages. You feed it your real voice, your real stories, and your real transformation. The templates handle the design. The result sounds like you, not like a robot.
What if I do not know HTML or coding?
+
You do not need to. The templates are pre-built and ready to customize. You swap in your colors, your copy, and your links. The course shows you exactly how. If you can change text in a Google Doc, you can customize these templates.
What types of offers does this work for?
+
Tarot readings, energy healing sessions, coaching packages, group programs, courses, workshops, mentorships, and any service-based spiritual offer. The templates adapt to your specific modality and the prompting system adjusts based on how you describe your work.
Will my page look like everyone else who buys this?
+
No. There are 11 distinct templates, each with its own color palette, mood, and archetypal energy. You also customize the colors, copy, and imagery to match your brand. Two people using the same template will end up with completely different pages because their voice, their offer, and their aesthetic choices are different.
Why is this only $17?
+
Because every spiritual entrepreneur deserves access to professional-quality sales infrastructure regardless of where they are in their business. This is intentionally priced as a gateway. The templates alone would cost hundreds from a designer. The training would cost more as a standalone course. $17 removes every barrier between you and a sales page that actually converts.
How long does it take to get a finished sales page?
+
Most people can go from start to finished page in a single focused session of 2 to 4 hours. That includes watching the training, recording your answers, generating the copy, customizing the template, and setting up your payment link. Compare that to the weeks or months most people spend trying to figure it out alone.
Will this work on the free Claude account?
+
Yes. The entire system works on Claude's free plan. The free account has usage limits, so you may need to spread your session across a couple of sittings instead of completing everything in one go. The paid plan lets you move faster with higher limits, but it is absolutely not required. The prompts, the method, and the templates all work exactly the same either way.
Will the sales page cost me anything to host?
+
No. The course includes free solutions for everything: free hosting for your page, free booking setup through Calendly, and free payment processing setup through Stripe. The only cost you will encounter is standard credit card processing fees from Stripe when you actually make a sale, which is the same fee every business pays regardless of platform. There are zero monthly fees, zero subscription costs, and zero hidden charges to get your page live and accepting clients.
